1. Scope of Service

Amgele Anubandh Europe (hereafter referred to as "the Team") acts solely as a facilitator to help members of the Hindu Konkani Saraswat community in Europe identify potential matrimonial matches.

  • Facilitation Only: Our role is limited to providing a platform for discovery and facilitating initial connections.
  • No Mediation: The Team is not responsible for taking the alliance forward, mediating discussions, or organizing family meetings. All further steps are the sole responsibility of the candidates and their respective families.

2. Accuracy & Verification Disclaimer

  • Trust Basis: The Team operates entirely on a Good Faith/Trust Basis.
  • No Vetting: We do not independently verify the accuracy of the data provided (including education, occupation, or residency status) nor do we vouch for the character, background, or medical history of any candidate.
  • Due Diligence: Candidates and their families are strictly advised to perform their own independent background checks and due diligence before finalising any alliance.

3. Data Privacy & Responsible Usage (GDPR)

In accordance with EU GDPR guidelines, by accessing information about other candidates, you agree to:

  • Purpose Limitation: Use the shared data only for the intended purpose of matrimonial inquiry.
  • Confidentiality: You shall not share, screenshot, or distribute another candidate's profile, photos, or contact details to any third party or social media platform without explicit consent.
  • Data Security: Any information received must be stored securely and deleted if you decide not to pursue a match.

4. Mandatory Reporting & Updates

To maintain the integrity of our database and respect the time of other participants, you agree to:

  • Marriage Fixation: Inform the Team immediately if your marriage is fixed (whether through this platform or elsewhere) so that your profile can be ruled out from future matches.
  • Periodic Updates: The Team appreciates periodic updates regarding ongoing communications, lack of interest, or the discontinuation of talks with a suggested profile. This helps us refine your future matches.

5. Limitation of Liability

The Team shall not be held liable for any emotional distress, financial loss, or disputes arising from interactions, fraudulent information, or the eventual outcome of a match.
